A teacher's patience, a storyteller's voice.

I'm Eloisa, an education student who has spent her time in the classroom, both on-screen and in person. I've taught English online to Chinese students and face to face with Filipino learners, and I've learned that good teaching is mostly about listening: to how a student speaks, where they hesitate, and what makes them light up.

That same ear is what I bring to narration and voice-over work. I read with clear pronunciation, a steady pace, and a warm tone that helps listeners follow along, whether it's a children's story, a lesson, or a script that needs a calm, friendly voice.
